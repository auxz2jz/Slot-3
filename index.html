How the box decision works

The vision model identifies a common object such as “cell phone,” “bottle,” “book,” “car,” or “dog.” The app compares that label and its broad category with the names and descriptions you gave your boxes. If you correct the box by tapping it, that object-to-box choice is remembered for future photos.

If your boxes are only named “Box 1,” “Box 2,” etc. with no descriptions, the app has no way to know your personal organization system on the first encounter. Tap the correct box once and it will learn that recognized object.
